## `lib/maturity.ts` is the ONE maturity rule (features-service#1196; supersedes OUTCOME_LAG_DAYS and the 10-outcome bar)
Why: a young workflow's flash price read cheap, took the budget, then its own spend floor passed the next (a ladder: $357 of $1,022 on 33 zero-reply workflows).
- **Two versions of every figure**: FLASH = all to date; MATURE = spend of runs STARTED before the cutoff over the outcomes (whenever they land) of the leads those runs served. One clock: lead-service `lastServedAt` (never `leads_campaigns.sent_at`); no serve date = IN.
- **Per leg**: `start_to_conversation` 21 days/1 positive reply; `start_to_website_visit` 21 days/10 visits; others 0 days/10 (mature ≡ flash). Cutoff = UTC midnight today − duration. Published on `/public/channels` `legs[].maturity`. `outcomesRequired` is also the learning bar; `MIN_MEASURED_FROM_REACHED` (10) is separate.
- **Ranking**: a workflow whose MATURE fleet outcomes on the leg reach the bar is priced on MATURE evidence at EVERY grain, cascade included (`row.maturity.basis`); else flash + cascade floor (exploration; the floor-as-exploration rule applies to FLASH rows only). The fleet verdict never REMOVES a grain (2026-10-01, osprey's 2-day mission vanished): a crossOrg/brand/campaign/offer grain of a mature row with no mature evidence is served on its FLASH block (`basis:"flash"`, own `isMature:false`); `resolved` stays the mature ladder's. The AUDIENCE grain stays mature-only (campaign-service's audience draw reads its evidence). Actual-cost overlay keys known-ness on each grain's own `basis`. Guards `routes/workflow-projection-maturity.test.ts`, `lib/actual-cost-projection.test.ts`.
- **`priceByGrain` = the price HELD per grain on BOTH bases** (`heldPriceOn`, leg reads, every row kind): own block's floored `legOutcome.costPerOutcomeUsd` on that version's ladder, else the NEAREST coarser grain's (`inherited` + `fromGrain`), else null + `unpricedReason`. The dashboard reads it, never re-walks the cascade; observed blocks/`resolved`/rank untouched. Actual-cost: known per `fromGrain`, else `vendor_cost_unknown`. Guards in `workflow-projection-maturity.test.ts`, `workflow-projection-actual-cost.test.ts`.
- **Mature counts are PERSONS** (`lib/mature-evidence.ts`); mature spend summed exactly (`lib/decimal.ts`).
- **Fail to FLASH, never to a false mature, never to a 502**: a row without `lastServedAt` or a failed mature read → `maturity.measured:false` (`serve_dates_unavailable`/`mature_evidence_unavailable`), mature null, loud log.
- Serving: leg-keyed reads only (funnel/goal bodies byte-unchanged). Every grain block `{basis, flash, mature, isMature}`; every row `maturity`. Observed figures are never floored.
- Guards: `lib/maturity.test.ts`, `routes/workflow-projection-maturity.test.ts`, `lib/fleet-positive-repliers.test.ts`.

## `learningPhase` (features-service#938; rides the maturity verdict, see lib/scope-maturity)
- Five verdicts: `priced`, `learning` (countdown), `learning_limited` (spend target reached, outcomes not arrived; NOT terminal: outcomes lag ~21 days, `outcomeLagDays`), `paused`, `unmeasured` (+ `unmeasuredReason`, one of seven, e.g. `no_expected_price`, `no_campaigns`, `no_daily_ceiling`, `leg_unpriceable`). None is a zero.
- **Price = Σ spend ÷ Σ outcomes over the (campaign × workflow) CELLS THAT OBSERVED an outcome** (exploration spend on barren workflows is not the price of an outcome). Do NOT simplify to whole spend / whole count, and do NOT use the cheapest workflow (an explore floor) or the stored campaign workflow. No cell observed one → `unmeasured`, never a floor. Outcome = the campaign's own leg `toStep`.
- Countdown subject = LEADING LIVE campaign (most outcomes, id tiebreak); a paused campaign is never the subject; `campaigns[]` lists all. `committedSpentUsd` read from the runs LEDGER (`groupBy=campaignId`), equals `costEconomics.committedCostUsd`, not summed cells. Priced against billing's per-leg ceiling (`GET /internal/brands/:id/legs/:legKey/daily-budget`; null = no ceiling ≠ 0); `ceilingScenarios` (2×/3×/5×) are served. Rides `RevenueBody` (campaign/offer/brand grains); NULL on cold start, lens, lean `groupBy`; FAIL-SOFT to a named `unmeasured`. Guards `lib/learning-phase.test.ts`, `routes/learning-phase-grain.test.ts`.
