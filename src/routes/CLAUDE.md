- Vocabulary = lead-service's own: `outreach` (customer says our outreach caused it), `other` (a real deal, theirs), `unstated` (NOBODY was asked: pre-field statements, tracker outcomes). `?cause=` is a comma set, canonicalised (one cache cell), unrecognised/empty → 400 `cause_unrecognised`; tracker words `attributed/needs_review/unmatched` REFUSED. - **Default (owner 2026-09-25) = `outreach` alone is PRICED**; the old all-states default is `?cause=outreach,other,unstated`. - Every money grain moved at once (`/features/:slug/revenue` all shapes, `/brands/:id/revenue|offers`, `/offers/:id/revenue`); rides every `scope_key` (`causeScopeKeyPart` keys every set, default included). ## Brand / offer / channel grains (`/brands/:id/{revenue,audience-stats,pipeline-activity,offers}`, `/offers/:id/{revenue,audience-stats,pipeline-activity,outcomes}`)
